# One external model provider, ZDR, routed ladder

Exactly one external model flow: the Anthropic API, direct, under an org zero-data-retention configuration in force **before the first real rep content reaches the API** (models requiring 30-day retention excluded). Web research uses Anthropic server-side web tools on this flow. Routing by the routine/high-stakes classification: Haiku 4.5 (routine drafts, classification, extraction), Sonnet 5 (high-stakes drafts, Q&A, workflows, owner twin), Opus 5 (rare hard steps). Extraction on the Batch API. Embeddings (BGE-M3) and transcription (faster-whisper) are self-hosted rep-side. Prompt-cache discipline: stable prefixes, per-twin style profile as a cached block.

Chosen so rep content never spreads across model vendors and retention terms can't contradict the erasure story.

**Amended 2026-09-02 (Dito-Core/dito#148): extraction moves from Haiku 4.5 to Sonnet 5.** The ladder above put extraction on the routine rung — *"Haiku 4.5 (routine drafts, classification, extraction)"* — on the routine/high-stakes classification. Three live sessions then filed a wrong delivery date from a relative weekday (#57, #69, #102), and #139 traced the cause: the turn the model receives was captured verbatim and shown to be correct — the rep's day, its weekday, its zone, and every beat stamped with the day it was said on — so the error was Haiku 4.5's own arithmetic on a correct calendar, not a rendering fault. Prose could not fix it: a prompt paragraph stating the convention was measured, made the target case no better and three working cases worse, and was withdrawn on the owner's ruling. The same five chats on Sonnet 5 got every weekday right.

**What the axis missed.** Extraction is routine by VOLUME — one batch call per bound chat per day, off the NFR-2 path — and that is what put it on the bottom rung. It is not routine by DIFFICULTY: a date is the one field of a candidate fact that the rep is asked to check and that no downstream gate can catch, since #66 measured every wrong crossing arriving at 0.95–1.00 confidence. Volume and difficulty are different questions, and the ladder had only asked one of them.

**The rung, as it now stands.** Extraction runs on `claude-sonnet-5` by default, with `DITO_EXTRACTION_MODEL` as the override and `DITO_EXTRACTION_EFFORT` selecting `output_config.effort` (unset = the model's own adaptive thinking).

**The entity-kind classification does NOT move with it, and this is the correction that mattered most.** It was going to: the runtime derived the kind model from the extraction config, on this ADR's own reasoning that a two-valued classification sits on the extraction rung — so pointing extraction at Sonnet 5 re-pointed a second call site silently, at both the startup backlog and the live path where a rep confirms a binding. `runEntityKindHarness` is not the harness this ticket fixed: it still runs on 256 max tokens with a second submission refused by counting, which is precisely what adaptive thinking breaks, and a failed determination fail-stops the consumer and leaves the confirmed entity unfiled. So the kind classification is now an independent rung with its own default, `claude-haiku-4-5` — the model it was actually measured on — and no environment override at all, because a knob whose safe range is one value is a hazard rather than a setting. Moving it is its own ticket: raise the budget, make the stop structural, then measure what the two-valued classification gains. Two harness limits in the EXTRACTION harness had to go first, and both were the reason the lane could not measure this for itself: `max_tokens` was 2,048, which Sonnet 5's thinking block consumed in full, and the one-submission rule was enforced by refusing the model's second call rather than by stopping the runner after the first. Nothing else on the ladder moves; drafting and Q&A were already Sonnet 5, and Opus 5 is still unused.

**Amended 2026-09-02 (Dito-Core/dito#154): the entity-kind classification moves to Sonnet 5 too — the paragraph above is superseded on WHERE the rung sits, and stands on WHY it could not move then.** What held it back was never a measurement saying Haiku 4.5 classified better; it was that `runEntityKindHarness` still carried the three limits #148 had just removed from extraction — `max_tokens: 256`, three runner iterations, and a second submission refused by counting the model's calls. Those are limits that only a model which answers immediately can live inside, and the failure they would have produced is the one the paragraph above describes: no determination at all, the consumer fail-stopped, and the entity the rep had just confirmed left unfiled. #154 gave the harness the same three fixes — 16,000 tokens of room, `max_iterations: 1` so the second round is never offered rather than refused after the fact, and the counting kept only for the one path a single round can still carry (two `tool_use` blocks in one message, which is two kinds with nothing to choose between them).

**No measurement stands behind the move, and that is the owner's ruling rather than an omission** — recorded on #154 as «no measurement, just move it to Sonnet 5». The paragraph above left "measure what the two-valued classification gains" as the price of moving this rung; that price was waived, not paid. So nothing here claims Sonnet 5 classifies better, and a measurement is what should move the rung again in either direction.

**The entity-kind rung, as it now stands.** The classification runs on `claude-sonnet-5` by default, with `DITO_ENTITY_KIND_MODEL` as the override — the knob #148 removed as a hazard when the harness could not survive being turned. It remains a SEPARATE rung: it does not read `DITO_EXTRACTION_MODEL` and is not derived from the extraction config, so the two rungs naming one model today is a coincidence of the ladder rather than a coupling, and the next rung to move moves alone. Drafting and Q&A were already Sonnet 5; Opus 5 is still unused; Haiku 4.5 is now on no rung this build routes.
