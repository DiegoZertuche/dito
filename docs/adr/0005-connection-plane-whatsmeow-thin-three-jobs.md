# Connection plane: whatsmeow, thin, three jobs

The connection plane is a Go service embedding whatsmeow that does exactly three jobs: (1) **filtered ingest** — write inbound events to `wa_events` with DEK-encrypted payloads, after the capture guard (ADR-0023); (2) **send execution** — execute `send_commands` per ADR-0006; (3) **rail status** — emit `rail_status` events powering the connection/send/onboarding state machines. Zero business logic; guard rules come from metadata-only config tables; the rail is a per-number selection behind this seam (ADR-0017). NFR-1 SLO: 99.5% monthly Dito-attributable session availability per seat; reconnect-with-history-sync p95 < 5 min; any session down > 10 min pages the operator.

Chosen because business logic leaking into the availability-critical plane turns a rail swap into a system rewrite.

**Amendment 2026-09-09 (a fourth job, and it is still not business logic).** Slice 6a gave the plane things to do that are asked of it rather than delivered to it (spec [#214](https://github.com/Dito-Core/dito/issues/214) decision 7; [#222](https://github.com/Dito-Core/dito/issues/222); boundary ([#225](https://github.com/Dito-Core/dito/issues/225))): generate a **pairing code** for the rep's number ([ADR-0031](0031-dashboard-notifies-twin-thread-is-a-pull-surface.md)), **fill** a chat's last ninety days on scope-in, and stop at a **scope-out** ([ADR-0023](0023-capture-governance-at-the-socket.md) as amended). Each is a **request row on the seam** the runtime writes and the plane takes and settles with exactly one outcome — served, refused with a named reason, or failed — under a budget of its own, so a request left taken and unsettled is the one state the table must not hold while its server lives. The plane decides nothing about *whether*: the runtime wrote the allowlist row in the rep's own act before it asked, and the guard is what it always was. That is job (1) and job (3) asked for on demand, not a fourth kind of logic, and it keeps the plane thin in the sense that matters — a rail swap replaces the library calls under each request and nothing above them. Two facts the boundary fixed: the fill **stamps its stop on the directory row** — the instant and the class, its final page and its stop in one statement — because the dashboard may read the directory and may not read the requests, and a card that cannot see the stop lies ([#245](https://github.com/Dito-Core/dito/issues/245)); and the pairing hello sends the **documented Browser (OS) display once** — the composed «Dito (Web)» was refused by the library on every ask by construction, and the Linked-devices name is what the rep types in WhatsApp's own prompt, so no property here decides it ([#222](https://github.com/Dito-Core/dito/issues/222), [#245](https://github.com/Dito-Core/dito/issues/245)).
