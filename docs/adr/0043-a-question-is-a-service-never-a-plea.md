# A question is a service, never a plea

**The twin asks the rep a question only when the answer makes the rep's own work smaller. It never asks to fill a hole in the record.** Diego's sentence at the slice-5b boundary, ruling on a question the rig had produced: *«we shouldn't be putting our limitations on the user»*. The extractor had read a customer's «lo mismo» two ways, submitted the reading it preferred at 0.7, and the confidence gate turned that into a question about goods the customer never named. Every word of the question was the system asking the rep to finish its work. Under this decision that question does not exist: a candidate below the gate — `stated` or `resolved_reference` — goes where an unresolved reference already goes, **dropped and counted**, no fact, no question, no line. The count stays on the run record so the price of precision has a denominator.

**Why the gate cannot be the source of a question.** [ADR-0039](0039-the-twin-asks-only-what-the-rep-can-settle.md) drew the line at *can the rep's answer settle the thing*, and a below-threshold candidate passes that test — the sentence is whole and the rep knows whether it is true. The test was right and incomplete. It asks whether the rep *can* answer; it does not ask whether answering is the rep's work or ours. A confidence number is a statement about the extractor's certainty, which is a property of our system, and every question the gate produces is a request that the rep spend attention repairing it. Two candidates that differ are not an answer with a low score; they are no answer, and asking the rep to pick is asking them to do the lookup the twin was built to do ([ADR-0039](0039-the-twin-asks-only-what-the-rep-can-settle.md)'s own fifth paragraph, applied to the case it did not reach). The rep's attention is the one budget this product cannot overspend ([ADR-0036](0036-no-architecture-tax-on-reps-or-owners.md)), and the gate was spending it on our uncertainty.

**What a question is, then.** A question is a capability of the twin, used when the answer shrinks the rep's day — *«¿le mando la cotización de ayer a Torres, que ya preguntó dos veces?»* — and the test for every future ask is the principle above, applied before the operative test of ADR-0039 rather than after it. The binding question survives on its own footing: the chat↔entity binding is one of the two acts reserved to the rep ([ADR-0037](0037-the-twin-talks-like-a-coworker.md) as amended; a wrong yes cannot be taken back), so asking it is not the twin filling a hole but the rep taking an act only the rep may take. Nothing else on the rep side asks today. When something does, it is a `question` part on a post ([ADR-0044](0044-the-post-primitive.md)) whose subject names the act a yes performs, and the act must be one the rep would have had to do anyway.

**What this retires.** The candidate-question machinery slice 5b built — the three-envelope question store, its posts, its answer tool, the disclosure submission's question part — lost its only source under the first paragraph and was retired in slice 6a ([#220](https://github.com/Dito-Core/dito/issues/220)), not left dormant: its tables are gone, and a binding question in flight became a post with a question part. [ADR-0003](0003-single-aperture-writer.md)'s class-1 sentence, *«below-threshold candidates become questions, never facts»*, now reads *«are dropped, never facts, never questions»*. [ADR-0039](0039-the-twin-asks-only-what-the-rep-can-settle.md)'s 2026-09-06 clause that a resolved candidate «may become a question» is superseded: a resolved candidate above the gate files exactly as a stated one, and below it drops exactly as a stated one. [ADR-0040](0040-a-customer-pattern-is-stated-never-inferred.md)'s citation of «the below-threshold route» as shipped behaviour is corrected in place. `CONTEXT.md`'s *Question* loses the below-threshold source.

Chosen because a surface that may interrupt the rep is only worth having if it never spends that permission on our own uncertainty — and the gate, by construction, produced nothing else. The cost is recall: a candidate the extractor was nearly sure of is lost rather than confirmed. FR-31 bought precision with recall on the day the fact types were chosen, and this is the same purchase at the surface the rep sees.
